## Elevator pitch

Ordel is a modern, premium-feeling Swedish social word game (in the spirit of
Wordfeud/Scrabble) for iPhone, built to respect players' time:

> Play a word. Put the phone away.

## Core promises

1. **Real people** — you know who you're playing.
2. **Clear word rules** — the game explains why a word is accepted or rejected.
3. **No nonsense** — no casino mechanics, currencies, energy systems, or
   manipulative engagement mechanics.
4. **Great word play** — the board, tiles, scoring and act of placing a word
   must feel excellent.

## Non-goals (see brief section 6 for the full list)

No coins, gems, energy, lives, loot boxes, paid gameplay advantages, or forced
engagement mechanics — ever. No monetization at all during V0.x beta.
